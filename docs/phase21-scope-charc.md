## The four web hazards 21-B/21-C must design against (CHARC, named at scoping)

1. **Double-click / refresh → two orders.** Needs a per-latch idempotency key, not merely a disabled button.
2. **GET/POST staleness TOCTOU.** The form renders a computed order; price moves; the click lands 90s later. Our own gotcha binds: emit the computation as a **hidden anchor** and validate the POST against that anchor's row — never silently recompute "latest" at POST time.
3. **Cancel targeting.** Cancelling by ticker is wrong with multiple/partially-filled orders — target a specific broker order id.
4. **Reconciliation distinguishability.** Framework-originated orders must be separable from operator-placed ones in the recon path (RD named this; it is also the D25-class audit requirement).

## RD's rulings (2026-07-27) — ADOPTED into scope

**Measurement: PRESERVED + the instrument STRICTLY IMPROVES; RD adopted the operator's design over his own tiers.** His decomposition of live-minus-shadow: (1) MECHANICAL NOISE (order never placed / wrong type / forgotten re-place / expiry-by-vacation) = contamination, not signal — the design REMOVES it; (2) DECISION VARIANCE (whether/when to act, stop choice, exit + partial timing, sizing) = the discipline signal — the design mechanizes NONE of it; (3) FILL QUALITY = a new measurement going live ADDS. His inversion of CHARC's counter: entry was ALREADY non-discretionary by the epoch contract, so mechanizing it IMPLEMENTS a committed rule rather than collapsing a decision — and **the form makes a non-entry LEGIBLE** (today a non-entry is ambiguous between decision and forgetting; FTRE proved that ambiguity costs a forensic to resolve).

**BINDING on 21-B — the three-state action space (operator-sharpened, RD-amended, and OPERATOR-RATIFIED in-session 2026-07-27 — a settled requirement of record, not a pending director proposal):** ACCEPT / DECLINE (+reason) / NO-ACTION — and **no-action must NOT default to "away"**, which would launder indecision into noise and bias the discipline signal OPTIMISTIC. Loop-closer: **(1) objective VIEW TELEMETRY** (was the panel viewed while the latch was armed — a fact, not a recollection) + **(2) ATTESTATION only for the ambiguous cell** (viewed=YES + no action → one terminal-state prompt; never prompt when telemetry says never-viewed). **The default for an unattested ambiguous cell is a DISCIPLINE LAPSE, not away** — pessimistic toward the operator, because an honest instrument does not flatter its subject. Acceptance test: FTRE 07-20 (vacation) must classify viewed=NO → AWAY → EXCLUDED from the discipline signal with its +1.22R in the away bucket.

**The strategic bonus RD named:** once away-fires are a distinct bucket, the **AWAY RATE becomes measurable** — which is the *quantified business case* that will justify or kill stage 3 (auto-place) on data rather than on how the FTRE miss felt.

**Stage-1 ledger shape (RD gates):** per latch event record — the framework's computed order + its derivation inputs (recomputable), the operator's actual action (clicked/declined+reason/manual, timestamped, with his actual params), the per-field framework-vs-actual DELTA (the execution-parity metric), outcome linkage, and order-validity outcome (stage 1 gets the FTRE broker-rejection class for free; stage 2's `preview_order` then eliminates it).

**Six derivation constraints for 21-A (RD):** (1) **FREEZE THE LATCH AT FIRE TIME** — never the current candidate row (live trap: FTRE's pivot drifted 18.34→18.59→20.19 *while armed*; VSTS's stop moved 12.90→13.27→13.40); (2) horizon in **SESSIONS not calendar days** (the 19-E ruling-A precedent); (3) **per-FIRE identity** — a latch is keyed to its fire, not its ticker (VSTS has fired twice); (4) fill detection **latch-specific**; (5) clear-reason **recorded not inferred** (fill/invalidation/horizon each stamped); (6) invalidation evaluated **on CLOSES**, not intraday touches.

## CHARC engineering assessment of the rulings (verified on disk 2026-07-27)

- **Constraints 1 + 3 are FREE — no schema needed.** `candidates` retains per-run rows (126 distinct evaluation runs) and each carries `pivot` + `initial_stop`; the A+ fires ARE those rows (verified: FTRE @ run 121 pivot **18.34** / stop 14.88 — the *frozen* value, while the current row shows the drift; VSTS @ run 126 pivot **16.90**, and VSTS @ run 99 pivot 13.56 as a SEPARATE row). So "freeze at fire time" = read the fire's own row and never a later one, and per-fire identity = key on `(evaluation_run_id, ticker)`. `pattern_detection_events` does NOT carry pivot/stop — the derivation must key on candidates, not the detection table.
- **View telemetry is NOT free** (RD's "essentially for free" is the one claim that doesn't hold): recording a view means **writing on a GET**, which our web layer does not do — it needs a deliberate seam (a beacon POST or an explicit view-log write) and storage. Sound requirement, real cost.
- **SEQUENCING TRAP (CHARC-found, nobody had named it): if the panel ships in 21-A without view telemetry, every latch fire between 21-A and 21-B is PERMANENTLY UNCLASSIFIABLE** — viewed=unknown makes away-vs-saw-it unresolvable exactly as RD's amendment forbids. Fires run ~weekly (FTRE 07-20, VSTS 07-27). **CHARC recommends folding the MINIMAL view-telemetry (one timestamp record) into 21-A**, accepting one small table there rather than a blind window at the instrument's start. Alternative if the operator prefers 21-A stay no-schema: fires in the gap are explicitly stamped **pre-telemetry** and excluded honestly — never defaulted.
- **Shadow-twin linkage resolves cleanly, no persistence needed:** the engine persists NOTHING to the DB (pure recompute → ephemeral artifacts, and per the Phase-19 Option-C ruling those are ignored except cited ones). So do NOT persist the shadow side — persist the **fire identity** (`evaluation_run_id`+ticker / detection identity); the current artifact always contains the twin because the engine recomputes the full corpus every run. The join happens at RD's read time.

- **Finding 3 RULED: FOLD THE MINIMAL TELEMETRY INTO 21-A.** His reasons: the gap fires are the instrument's FIRST fires (they anchor the ledger's opening baseline); unclassifiable is LOST data, not uncertain data, under his own do-not-flatter rule; and ~weekly cadence makes it 1–3 permanently blind fires against one small table — not a close call.
- **Finding 4 CONCUR + REFINEMENT: store BOTH identities.** The candidate fire identity `(evaluation_run_id, ticker)` freezes pivot/stop, but the shadow artifact keys on `(ticker, detection_date)` and those are **different id spaces** (`evaluation_runs` vs `pipeline_runs`) — storing only the evaluation identity makes the shadow join derivable-by-convention rather than exact. Store the detection identity explicitly too: *cheap now, unrecoverable later.*

**CHARC ruling on RD's schema cost-sharing suggestion (my lane):** the 0032 taxonomy-type rider stays a **SEPARATE migration**, not merged into 21-A's. Sharing a migration couples unrelated concerns (a revert of one drags the other; the migration's purpose muddies) — the real saving RD identified is the *discipline* being exercised once, which is a sequencing benefit, not a merge requirement. 21-A takes its own minimal migration; 0032 rides 21-B or a lull with its own.

## The measurement question (RD's lane — ROUTED + RULED 2026-07-27)

RD argued full mechanization would END the operator-discipline half of the H1 measurement (live becomes "shadow with real fills"). **CHARC's read: this design does NOT — the operator still decides whether and when to act; only the arithmetic and the memory are mechanized.** That strictly improves the measurement by removing mechanical noise (the FTRE divergence) while preserving the decision signal. But it is RD's ruling, and it should be a deliberate decision rather than an automation side-effect. His answer may reshape 21-B/21-C.
