## 1. The shape after D84

- **Bucket.** `bucket_for(trend_template_results, vcp_results, config)`. The risk pre-skip is gone and so
  is the parameter (E1). The criterion still computes and persists. A `risk_feasibility` fail is a
  displayed FLAG, never a bucket or gate outcome.
- **Risk passes.** `risk_layer_passes(RiskInputs)` in `swing/evaluation/scoring.py`, beside
  `structural_gate_passes`. It is defined once over a reduced form, with thin adapters (E2). "Pass" means
  every risk-LAYER result is `pass`, and `na` is a non-pass (RD (vi)).
- **Cohorts.** H1 = `aplus` AND risk passes. H4 = `aplus` AND risk does not pass. The old
  `watch|skip, non-pass == {risk_feasibility}` form is DELETED (RD). A pre-boundary-shaped `skip` row
  matches no hypothesis.
- **The one-share risk and the stated zero.** `SizingResult` carries `risk_per_share` (CHARC F1 (3)).
  ONE formatter in `sizing.py` renders the stated-zero line from a `SizingResult`, naming the basis it
  sized on. It appears at every surface listed in E3.
- **Latch (F1-A).** A risk-withheld card states its zero with the derivation, and it offers a NEW attest
  affordance (`acted_manually` plus the broker order id). A zero-share framework order stays UNWRITABLE
  (0033 `:416`, `:677-679`).
- **Watchlist.** `risk_feasibility` leaves `STABLE_CRITERION_NAMES` (F3).
- **Metrics.** The capital tile says "flagged" where it said "blocked". The field name and the value are
  unchanged (F4).
